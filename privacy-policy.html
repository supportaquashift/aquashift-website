Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WHS Auto Solution Private Limited, operating as AQUASHIFT ("Aquashift", "we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you visit theaquashift.in or book a doorstep car wash or car care service with us.

1. Information We Collect

When you book a service through our website, WhatsApp, or phone, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • Location or address where the service will be performed
  • Booking details, such as the service requested and your preferred date and time
  • Any additional information you choose to share in the message field of our contact form

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Confirm and schedule your booking
  • Send our technician to the correct location at the agreed time
  • Contact you about your booking, including confirmations, delays, or rescheduling
  • Improve our services based on booking patterns and feedback

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Your details are shared only with the Aquashift technician assigned to your booking, so they can locate you and carry out the service. 6. Your Rights

7. Data Security

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for use by adults booking car care services. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
